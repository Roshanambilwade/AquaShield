## Formula and semantic comparisons

The actual configured confidence weights match section 10: **35/25/20/10/10**. Spatial precision is discounted for locality-center or low-accuracy locations. The configured severity weights match section 11: **25/20/20/15/10/10**, thresholds **30/60/80**, duration maximum **25h**, population maximum **750**, temperature **25–44°C**, vulnerability maximum ratio **0.25**. Missing components remain null, with known contribution and a possible upper bound; they are not measured zero. Changing these is not authorized merely because illustrative inputs imply 91.

Population uses one contribution per internal reporter key, divided by assumed reporting coverage (default **0.25**), bounded by an area population estimate when present and never below the reported-household sum. Those assumptions and keys do not verify identities or deduplicate a census across multiple accounts/events. Aggregated event estimates may overlap and must not be labeled physically served people.

Operational allocation uses current ACTIVE need, supporting valid fresh evidence, current candidate/operator/quantity/reservation and recorded delivery context, with the unchanged severity-minus-delivery adjustment. Candidate selection prefers sufficient available quantity with least excess, otherwise the largest available quantity, with identifier tie-breaking; it does not optimize travel distance. Distance/ETA are computed separately for logistics after assignment and remain estimates. Distance-aware fleet optimization is PARTIAL relative to the original goal, not silently added in this audit. AI cannot change the authoritative ranking or quantities.

Numerical predictions match section 58 and `docs/phase8.md`: count cap four times median; baseline N−4 windows; alpha0.5 EWMA of recent four; bounded trend/persistence/recurrence weights **50/35/15**; thresholds **25/50/75**; forecast bounded at twice baseline with supported horizon equal to the configured window. At least **18** eligible records, **6** keys and all **12** complete positive six-hour UTC windows are required by defaults. Insufficiency remains null; no real predictive accuracy is established.
